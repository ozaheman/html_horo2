**Reference charts** (both Mumbai, IST UTC+05:30): 18 September **1981**, **01:20 AM**; and 7 October **1977**, **11:45 PM**. This report uses the two supplied screenshots as primary chart fixtures, not estimates from a different ayanamsa.

## 1. KP data validation

The screenshots contain **9 traditional grahas and 12 cusps each** (42 entries). All **42/42** sign / nakshatra / sub / sub-sub lord chains reproduced the same labels using the proportional Vimshottari subdivision algorithm. This confirms the subdivisions *given those longitudes*. Independent Swiss Ephemeris KP-ayanamsa calculations generally differ from the screenshot planet positions by 0–3.4 arcminutes and from house cusps by up to about 1.6 arcminutes with Mumbai set at 19.076 N, 72.8777 E. Subdivision membership close to a boundary is sensitive to such differences: to reproduce the exact screenshot use its degrees or ensure the precise provider, coordinates, ayanamsa, and node choice agree.

The app previously used **equal-house** natal cusps in core KP calculations; it now prefers actual **Placidus** cusps for the active natal D1. For the supplied algorithm, a planet's house numbers are **union of** (i) planet occupied house, (ii) houses whose cusp sign the planet rules, (iii) planet's nakshatra-lord occupied house, and (iv) houses whose cusp sign the nakshatra lord rules. Four-grade house significators are a **separate** ranking: A planets in occupant's star, B occupants, C planets in cusp lord's star, D cusp sign lord. Reverse CSL-of-cusp numbers are **not** planetary significations and now have their own diagnostic function.
