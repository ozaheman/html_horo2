# Krishnamurti Paddhati (KP) Astrology: Procedure, Method, Understanding & Prediction

*Based on the pages you uploaded (Jyotish Sobati, pp. 258-270: the "Krishnamurti Paddhati" intro and the "Ansh Phale" tables by sign), plus standard KP practice. Marathi terms are kept in brackets.*

---

## 1. What is KP?

- Developed by **Late K. S. Krishnamurti**. Most traditional astrology rules still apply.
- The key difference: the zodiac is divided **according to the Vimshottari Dasha system**, not just into signs and nakshatras.
- Each nakshatra (13°20' = 800') is split into **9 unequal parts**, in proportion to each planet's Vimshottari years (out of 120). The part's ruler is the **Sub Lord** (सब लॉर्ड).
- The Sub Lord is the **decisive factor** in KP. Whether a house's result is favourable or not depends on its sub lord.

### The three layers of any degree in the zodiac

| Layer | Marathi | Tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Sign Lord | राशी स्वामी | Basic nature / context |
| Star Lord | नक्षत्र स्वामी | What the planet *will give* (the house results it carries) |
| Sub Lord | सब लॉर्ड | **Final verdict**: whether the result happens, and how |

Written in the tables as e.g. **मं. के. के.** = Mars (sign) - Ketu (star) - Ketu (sub).

## 2. The 249 divisions

- 27 nakshatras x 9 sub-divisions = 243.
- 6 extra divisions occur where a nakshatra straddles a sign boundary and the sub is cut in two. **Total = 249**.
- The book's tables list all 249 rows (numbered 1-249) with, for each: Sign-Star-Sub lord, **nature/qualities** (स्वभाव, गुण), **diseases** (रोग) and **profession** (व्यवसाय).

### Sub length of each planet inside a nakshatra (800' basis)

Formula: `Sub length = (planet's dasha years / 120) x 800'`

| Planet | Dasha yrs | Sub length |
|---|---|---|
| Ketu | 7 | 0° 46' 40" |
| Venus | 20 | 2° 13' 20" |
| Sun | 6 | 0° 40' 00" |
| Moon | 10 | 1° 06' 40" |
| Mars | 7 | 0° 46' 40" |
| Rahu | 18 | 2° 00' 00" |
| Jupiter | 16 | 1° 46' 40" |
| Saturn | 19 | 2° 06' 40" |
| Mercury | 17 | 1° 53' 20" |

*(The printed page shows Saturn as 2°6'20"; the mathematically correct value is 2°6'40". The nine parts add to exactly 13°20' only with 40".)*

### Order of sub lords
Within a nakshatra, the first sub belongs to the **star lord**, then the rest follow in Vimshottari order:

**Ketu → Venus → Sun → Moon → Mars → Rahu → Jupiter → Saturn → Mercury → (repeat)**

Example from the Aries page: Ashwini (Ketu's star) starts with Ketu sub (0°-0°46'40"), then Venus sub, then Sun sub, and so on.

## 3. Core rules of KP (from the book's intro, p. 258)

1. **Sub lord is the decider.** A house cusp's sub lord shows whether that house's matters are favourable or not.
2. **A planet gives the results of its star lord too.** Example: Mars (4th and 9th lord) in the lagna, placed in the Mrigashira / Chitra / Dhanishtha stars, will give the results of the house it owns plus its own placement, and the planets in Mars's stars give results of the 4th, 9th and 1st houses in their dashas/antardashas. The favourable or unfavourable nature follows the planet's sub lord.
3. **Bhavarambh (house cusp) system** is used to compute houses (cusps, not equal-house).
4. **Rahu and Ketu** give the results of the **sign lord** they occupy (more strongly), as well as their star lord and any planet they are conjunct with.
5. **Check the supporting houses, not only the main house.** For an event, study the dasha/antardasha of planets connected to all the supporting houses.
   *Example:* for children, look at the 5th house, but for *obtaining* children also the 2nd (family) and 11th (gains).
6. **Before analysing a chart, prepare a planet table** with, for each planet: 
   1. which houses it **owns**, 
   2. which house it **occupies**, 
   3. its **star lord** and which houses that star lord owns/occupies, 
   4. which houses it **aspects**, 
   5. which planets it is **conjunct** with (it takes their characteristics).
7. Write the **sign lord, star lord and sub lord** next to each planet and each cusp.
8. Some scholars also use the **sub-sub lord**.
9. Sign, star and sub together give the full 249-part division; this is also used in **Prashna (horary)** charts (numbers 1-249).
10. **Ayanamsa:** KP uses its own ayanamsa (close to the Chitrapaksha/Lahiri value, differing by a few arc-minutes). Use KP ayanamsa in your software.
11. From the 249 divisions, fixed indications can be given: **nature, disease and profession**, which is what the tables in this book list.

## 4. Step-by-step procedure

### Step 1: Collect data
Date, exact time and place of birth (or the time the question is asked, for Prashna). Time accuracy matters because the sub lord changes within minutes of arc.

### Step 2: Cast the chart
- Use **KP ayanamsa**, **Placidus** house system (bhavarambh cusps), Vimshottari dasha.
- Get planet longitudes and **12 cusp longitudes** (degree-minute-second).

### Step 3: Make the KP table
For every planet and every cusp, find: **Sign lord / Star lord / Sub lord** (and optionally sub-sub lord).

### Step 4: Find significators of each house
Use the standard 4-level KP ranking (strongest first):

1. Planets **in the star of the occupant** of the house
2. The **occupant** of the house
3. Planets **in the star of the owner** (lord) of the house
4. The **owner** of the house

Rahu/Ketu are added where they sit in the sign of the lord, or are in conjunction/aspect with the relevant planet (rule 4 above).

### Step 5: Pick the right cusp for the question
Check the **cusp sub lord** of the main house. Then see which houses that sub lord signifies:

- If the sub lord signifies the houses supporting the event → the event **will happen**.
- If it signifies the opposing houses → it will be **denied or delayed**.

### Step 6: Check timing
1. The **dasha lord, antardasha lord (and pratyantar)** must be significators of the supporting houses.
2. Use **transits** (esp. Jupiter, Saturn, Sun, Moon) over the sub lord/star lord for exact timing.
3. Use **Ruling Planets (RP)** at the moment of judgment as a confirmation tool (see §7).

### Step 7: Use the 249-part table for character, health and profession
Find the Ascendant (lagna) longitude (and other cusp longitudes) in the tables; read the row (see §6).

## 6. How to use the "Ansh Phale" tables (pp. 259-270)

### Columns

| Column | Marathi | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| क्र. | Number | Row 1-249 across the zodiac |
| अं.क.वि. | Degree-Minute-Second | **Start** of this sub-division *within the sign* |
| (below it) | Lords | Sign lord · Star lord · Sub lord |
| स्वभाव, गुण | Nature | Personality traits |
| रोग | Diseases | Health tendencies |
| व्यवसाय | Profession | Career indications |

### Rule to read the row
A row applies from its own **अं.क.वि.** to the **start of the next row** (the book says planets/cusps falling anywhere in that span come under that row's lords).

### Worked method
1. Take the **Ascendant** longitude, e.g. Taurus 6°10'.
2. Go to the Taurus page (p. 260). Rows start at 0°00'00", 1°13'20", 3°00'00", 5°06'40", 7°00'00" ...
3. 6°10' lies between **5°06'40"** and **7°00'00"**, so row **26** applies (Venus–Sun–Mercury: शु. र. बु.; in the book's abbreviations **र.** = Ravi/Sun and **रा.** = Rahu).
4. Read off the traits, diseases and professions in that row.
5. Do the same for other key points:
   - **Lagna** → nature / personality (स्वभाव)
   - **6th cusp** → diseases (रोग)
   - **10th cusp** → profession (व्यवसाय)
   - **4th / 7th cusp** → spouse's profession or nature (as the book hints)

## 7. Ruling Planets (RP): confirming and timing

At the moment of judgment (or the query), take:

1. Day lord
2. Moon's sign lord, star lord (and sub lord)
3. Lagna's sign lord, star lord (and sub lord)
4. Rahu/Ketu if they occupy those signs/stars

Planets that appear in the RP list **and** are connected to the supporting houses confirm the event and help fix the timing (together with dasha and transit).

## 8. Prediction workflow (quick checklist)

1. Define the question (marriage, job, health, etc.).
2. Pick the **main cusp** (e.g. 7th for marriage, 10th for career, 5th for children).
3. Find the **cusp sub lord**.
4. List the houses it signifies (via its star lord, own house, occupation).
5. Compare with **supporting** vs **denying** houses (§5).
6. Verdict: **Yes / No / Delayed**.
7. Find the timing: Dasha-Bhukti-Antara of planets linked to the supporting houses.
8. Refine with transits and Ruling Planets.
9. Add flavour from the 249-table: nature, health, profession of the relevant cusps.

---

## 9. Practical tips

- Always keep the **planet table** and **cusp table** with sign/star/sub lords side by side.
- Do not judge on a single planet; always judge using the **sub lord of the relevant cusp** first.
- A planet is only as good as its **sub lord** signifies.
- Avoid fatalistic statements; tables and rules are **indicative**.
- Check birth time with the help of RPs if it is uncertain (rectification).
- Use predictions as guidance, not as medical, legal or financial advice.
