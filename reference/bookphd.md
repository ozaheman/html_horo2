Business, status, respect, career, victory, fame yagya (fire sacrifice), livelihood, interest in work, manners (good or bad), departure, Authority (boss), good traits, sky etc. are considered from the tenth house. This is also called ‘Meshurana’ or the place of giving directions (orders).

Gains, income, receipts, incoming of persons, realisation- (siddhis), Riches (wealth-luxuries), welfare, praise, older brother or older sister, left ear, softness (glaze), good news etc. are to be considered from the eleventh house.

## Chesta Bala (Strength due to movement)

Now we explain the term “Chest Bala’. Sun and Moon are always direct in motion. They are never retrograde. The remaining Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Saturn are at times direct and at other times retrograde in motion. Due to their motion, they get bala and this bala is called Chesta Bala.

In order to calculate Chesta bala, an exhaustive mathematical methods are employed for knowing the centre of motion, slow speed etc. So it has not been explained here in detail. We are however mentioning some broad method to know the strength.

(a) When a planet is retrograde it gets 1 roopa (= 60 points). (b) Anuvakra (or Riju motion) then it is allotted 30 points. (c) If it is Vikal then 15 points. (d) Samagam motion then 30 points.
