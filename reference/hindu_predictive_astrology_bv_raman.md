# Reference: Hindu Predictive Astrology by B.V. Raman

## Overview
**Hindu Predictive Astrology** by B.V. Raman is a comprehensive work on Vedic astrology that bridges classical texts and practical predictions. B.V. Raman was a renowned Indian astrologer who wrote extensively on predictive techniques, Dasha systems, and house analysis.

## Key Prediction Rules from B.V. Raman

### 1. Predictive Astrology Fundamentals
- **Vedic Astrology** for predicting events based on planetary positions
- **Predictive Astrology** for timing events using Dasha and transits
- Combination of **Horoscopy** (birth chart) and **Transit** (current positions)

### 2. Overall Chart Assessment
- **Lagna (Ascendant)**: Represents the body, life force, and overall personality
- **Lagna Lord**: Indicates the general tone of life during a period
- **Lagna in Kendra/Trikona**: Strong chart, positive results
- **Lagna in Mara (6th/8th/12th)**: Weak chart, difficult life
- **Lagna Lord Afflicted**: Life situations dominated by conflict

### 3. Dasha Systems
- **Mahadasha**: Major life periods (7.2 years average)
- **Antardasha**: Sub-periods (1-5 years)
- **Pratyantardasha**: Sub-sub-periods
- **Sukshma Dasha**: Short-term events

### 4. Prediction of Marriage
- **7th House**: House of marriage and partnerships
- **7th Lord**: Indicates spouse's nature and marriage
- **Venus**: Best for marriage timing
- **Moon**: Early marriage if well-placed
- **Jupiter**: Happy married life, traditional marriage
- **Mars**: Conflicts, multiple marriages
- **Saturn**: Late marriage, difficult married life
- **Rahu**: Multiple marriages, divorce
- **Ketu**: Widowhood, short-lived marriage
- **1st (Lagna) aspects**: Marriage prospects

### 5. Prediction of Career
- **10th House**: Career, profession, reputation
- **10th Lord**: Professional fate
- **5th House**: Education, intellectual work
- **6th House**: Service, employment
- **2nd House**: Business, trade, income
- **5th/9th**: Finance, teaching, law
- **Planetary combinations**:
  - Jupiter in 10th = government/service
  - Saturn in 10th = technical/industrial work
  - Mercury in 10th = business, commerce
  - Venus in 10th = art, beauty, entertainment
  - Sun in 10th = authority, leadership

### 6. Prediction of Wealth
- **2nd House**: Family, wealth, speech
- **11th House**: Gains, income, hopes fulfilled
- **2nd Lord**: Wealth sources
- **11th Lord**: Income and gains
- **Dhana Yoga**: Wealth combination
  - Jupiter in 10th + 5th
  - Venus in 7th from 5th
  - 5th in 9th
  - Lagna in 9th

### 7. Health Predictions

- **12th House**: Expenses, foreign travel
