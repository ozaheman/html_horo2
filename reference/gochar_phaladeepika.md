# Reference: Gochar Phaladeepika Transits

## Overview
**Gochar Phaladeepika** (Transit Showers of Results) is a classical text on predictive astrology focusing on the effects of planetary transits (Gochar) over the natal chart. It explains how transiting planets influence different signs and houses.

## Key Prediction Rules from Gochar Phaladeepika

### 1. Transit Principles
- Each planet transits through all 12 signs sequentially
- Transit effects are strongest when planets transit their own sign
- Planets transit own sign = maximum effect (benefic = excellent; malefic = severe)
- Planets transit sign of exaltation = excellent results
- Planets transit sign of debilitation = poor results

### 2. 12-House Transits
- **1st House (Lagna)**: Self, health, reputation, fortune
- **2nd House**: Wealth, family, speech
- **3rd House**: Courage, siblings, short journeys, expression
- **4th House**: Mother, home, mental peace, property
- **5th House**: Intelligence, children, education, fortune
- **6th House**: Health, diseases, enemies, debts
- **7th House**: Spouse, partnerships, marriages, foreign travel
- **8th House**: Death, occult, sudden changes, inheritance
- **9th House**: Father, fortune, luck, dharma, guidance
- **10th House**: Career, profession, reputation, father
- **11th House**: Gains, income, hopes, fulfillment
- **12th House**: Foreign travel, expenses, loss, spirituality

### 3. Transit of Lagna Lord (Ascendant)
- When Lagna lord transits a sign, the affairs of that house become prominent
- 1st house lord transit = general well-being
- 2nd house lord transit = financial changes
- 3rd house lord transit = courage, siblings, short journeys
- 4th house lord transit = home, mother, family
- 5th house lord transit = education, children, wisdom
- 6th house lord transit = health issues, rivals
- 7th house lord transit = marriage, partnerships
- 8th house lord transit = death, occult
- 9th house lord transit = father, fortune
- 10th house lord transit = career, profession
- 11th house lord transit = gains, income
- 12th house lord transit = expenses, losses

### 4. Transits to Moon (Chandra)
- Moon's 1st house: general benefits
- Moon's 4th house: family, mother, emotional comfort
- Moon's 7th house: partnerships, marriage
- Moon's 10th house: career, status
- Moon's 11th house: gains, fulfillment

### 5. Special Transit Rules
- **Jupiter transit**: General growth, improvement, luck
- **Venus transit**: Pleasure, comfort, luxury, luxury spending
- **Mercury transit**: Communication, studies, work, business
- **Mars transit**: Conflict, energy, action, risks
- **Saturn transit**: Restrictions, discipline, delays, karma
- **Sun transit**: Authority, health, vitality, status
- **Rahu/Ketu transit**: Unexpected changes, spiritual shifts

### 6. Strong Transits
- Benefic planets in strong dignity give good results
- 2nd, 6th, 8th, 12th house from planet's position are weak (maras)
- Benefic in own sign = very good
- Benefic in exaltation or own sign in kendra = exceptional

### 7. Malefic Transits
- Saturn, Mars, Sun in own sign in 6th, 8th, 12th = severe
- Malefic in kendras and trikonas with aspect to Lagna = difficulties

### 8. Double Transit
- When a planet transits to the same sign as its dasha lord or its natal position
- Often amplifies the effect
- Double transit of Jupiter = exceptional fortune

### 9. Gochar Chintamani
- A specific text that details transit effects in detail

## Reference Sources
