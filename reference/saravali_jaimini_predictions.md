# Reference: Saravali - Jaimini Predictive Astrology

## Overview
**Saravali** (also spelled Saravali) is a classical text on Jaimini predictive astrology written by Shri Harshad Mehta. It is one of the most important texts on Ladla (horoscopy) and is considered a complementary work to Jaimini Upadesa Sutras. It contains detailed rules for predicting events based on planets, signs, houses, and their combinations.

## Key Prediction Rules from Saravali

### 1. Karaka (Significator) Principles
- **Atmakaraka** (Atmakaraka): The planet with the most degrees in a sign indicates the soul's purpose and life direction
- **Amatyakaraka** (AmK): Career, finances, advisors
- **Dharakara** (DK): Spouse, partnerships
- **Putrakaraka** (PK): Children, creativity
- **Matrikaraka** (MK): Mother, home
- **Bhratrikaraka** (BK): Siblings
- **Gnatikaraka** (GK): Enemies, disease, litigation
- **Pitrikaraka** (PiK): Father (in 8-karaka scheme)

### 2. Chara Dasha (Sign-Based Dasha)
- Derived from degree positions of planets in signs
- Direction based on Lagna: movedhri (anticlockwise) or avyantardhri (clockwise)
- Duration of each sign's dasha based on distance to the sign lord
- Scorpio/Aquarius: use stronger of two lords

### 3. Raja Yogas (Royal Yogas)
- **Rabhu Yoga**: Jupiter in 5th/9th from Lagna or its lord
- **Dhana Yoga**: Venus in 7th from 5th, or 5th lord in 7th/9th
- **Lakshmi Yoga**: Jupiter in 3rd from Lagna
- **Uchchaishwyapa Yoga**: Moon, Jupiter, Saturn aspects
- **Vashya Yoga**: Venus/Moon aspects to Lagna (through 2nd/12th)

### 4. Dashamladi (Pada-based Dasha)
- Based on navamsa division
- 27 padas divided among planets by portions
- Duration proportional to the portion

### 5. Transits (Gochar) Rules
- Each sign transitions through the 12 houses based on its sign placement
- Sign's natural karakas activate related houses
- Jupiter and Venus are benefic; Saturn, Mars, Sun, Mercury, Moon, Rahu, Ketu are malefic
- Benefic planets in lagna, 5th, 9th, 11th give good results; malefics in 6th, 8th, 12th cause suffering

### 6. Sambandha (Relationship) Rules
- Planets in each other's signs create relationships
- These relationships affect results of transits and dasha

### 7. Special Yogas
- **Ardha Chandra Yoga**: Moon in one of the two central degrees of a sign
- **Hamsa Yoga**: Moon in Leo (5th sign)
- **Makar Yoga**: Sun, Moon, Jupiter in same sign

### 8. Dasha Antardasha Rules
- Antardasha lord is the karaka of the sign occupied by the mahadasha lord
- Sub-periods within sub-periods follow similar rules

### 9. Assessment Principles
- Strength of planets = dignity (rashi, nakshatra, other signs)
- Placement in kendras (1,4,7,10) and trikonas (1,5,9) gives strength
- Placement in maras (6,8,12) gives weakness
- Benefic conjunct malefic = condition to be examined
- Own sign + friendly sign = strong; own sign + enemy sign = weakened

### 10. Dwadashamsha (D12) Chart
- House activation based on zodiac sign
- 12 houses for different life areas
- Planets in each house indicate results for that area
