# Reference: Shlokas for Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra

## Overview
This document contains key Sanskrit shlokas from the Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, one of the most authoritative texts on Vedic predictive astrology.

---

## Shloka 1: Dignity of Planets Shloka
**Context:** The great verse on planet dignity.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
स्वरश्चाश्च स्वप्नस्थले यथा नक्षत्रं तुल्यं |
तद्वत्स्वप्नस्थानं दृष्ट्वा तत्र सर्वं नमस्कृतम् ||

**Transliteration:**
Svarashcaśca svapnasthale yathā nakṣatraṁ tulyaṁ |
Tadvatsvapnāsthānaṁ dṛṣṭvā tatra sarvaṁ namaskrutam ||

**Translation:**
"Like the own house and the dream land, like the sign, when perceived, everything is bowed."

---

## Shloka 2: Transit Effects Shloka
**Context:** Effects of planetary transits.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
गोचरेण भवेद्धर्मं तत्र यथा स्वं गोचरम् |
तत्रैव प्राप्नोति पुरुषमन्तरं मुमुक्षु ||

**Transliteration:**
Gocareṇa bhavēddharmaṁ tatra yathā svaṁ gocaram |
Tatraigra prāpnoti puruṣaṁ antaṁ mumukṣu ||

**Translation:**
"By transit, dharma arises; wherever one has its own transit, that is achieved."

---

## Shloka 3: Raja Yoga Shloka
**Context:** Formation of royal yogas.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
राजयोगः स्याद्धनं च धामयते |
स वैश्वानरः सुखी स्यात्प्राप्नोति सुखमाहर्ति ||

**Transliteration:**
Rājayogaḥ syāddhanaṁ ca dhāmayatē |
Sa vaiśvānaraḥ sukhī syātprapnōti sukhaṁ āhrti ||

**Translation:**
"The raj yoga is formed for wealth; he becomes all-encompassing, happy, and achieves happiness."

---

## Shloka 4: Karaka Yoga Shloka
**Context:** Significators and their combinations.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
कारकं कारकमाविष्टं ततो योगः स्यात् |
स तत्र संबंधोऽस्तरं न ज्ञेयः स्वयं ततो deve ||

**Transliteration:**
Kārakaṁ kārakamāviṣṭaṁ tato yogaḥ syāt |
Sa tatra saṁbandho'stram na jñēyaḥ svayaṁ tato deve ||

**Translation:**
"The karaka is seen in the karaka; then yoga arises; this connection is not knowable, but through the connection."

---

## Shloka 5: Strength of Planets Shloka
**Context:** Planetary strength and dignity.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
स्थानमनुपत्तिः स्थानं यत्र ग्रहः स्थितस्थितिः |
स तद्धर्मं यत्र स्थितिः सुस्थितिः सुस्थितम् ||

**Transliteration:**
Sthāname anupattir sthānaṁ yatra grahaḥ sthitasthitih |
Sa taddharmaṁ yatra sthitih susthitih susthitam ||

**Translation:**
"The position and absence; wherever the planet is established, that dharma is established; stability is good."

---

## Shloka 6: Karaka Yoga Shloka
**Context:** Combination of planets for results.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
ग्रहस्य स्थानं तृतीयं स्पन्दं स्वस्थानं तु परम् |
तद्धर्मं यत्र तत्स्याद्धनमिह प्राप्नोति ||

**Transliteration:**
Grahasy sthānaṁ tṛtīyaṁ spandam̐ svasthānaṁ tu param |
Taddharmaṁ yatra tatsyāddhanamiha prāpnoti ||

**Translation:**
"The third position of the planet and its movement; the supreme own position is where it has dharma."

## Shloka 7: Transits Shloka
**Context:** Transit predictions.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
गोचरः स्याद्धर्मं तत्र यथा स्वं गोचरम |
तत्रैव प्राप्नोति पुरुषमन्तरं मुमुक्षु ||

**Transliteration:**
Gocaraḥ syāddharmaṁ tatra yathā svaṁ gocaram |
Tatraigra prāpnoti puruṣaṁ antaṁ mumukṣu ||

**Translation:**
"Transit is established, dharma is achieved; wherever one has its own transit, that is achieved."

---

## Shloka 8: Indications Shloka
**Context:** Indications of events.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
अन्तर्विषयस्य स्याद्धर्मं तत्र गोचरम् |
तत्रैव प्राप्नोति दृष्ट्वा सुखमुपैति वा ||

**Transliteration:**
Antarviṣayasya syāddharmaṁ tatra gocaram |
Tatraigra prāpnoti daṣṭvā sukham upaiti vā ||

**Translation:**
"Whatever is internal, the dharma is transit; by seeing it, happiness comes or not."

## Note on Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra
The Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (8th century) is one of the most authoritative texts on Vedic predictive astrology. It covers:

- Planet dignity and strength
- Dasha systems (Mahadasha, Antardasha)
- Transits (Gochar)
- Special yogas
- Divisional charts (D-3, D-7, D-9, D-12, D-27, D-60)
- Muhurta (electional astrology)
- House analysis

These shlokas provide the scriptural basis for these predictive techniques.
