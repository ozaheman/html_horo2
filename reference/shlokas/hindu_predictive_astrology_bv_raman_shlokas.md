# Reference: Shlokas for Hindu Predictive Astrology by B.V. Raman

## Overview
This document contains translated shlokas that represent the principles found in Hindu predictive astrology, particularly as articulated by B.V. Raman.

---

## Shloka 1: Spiritual Foundation Shloka
**Context:** The essence of predictive astrology.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
ज्योतिर्ब्रह्म्यं ज्ञानमीश्वरं तत्र योगस्तव |
तत्र नेता तद्विद्वान् न प्राप्नोति लोकं ||

**Transliteration:**
Jyotirbrahmyaṁ jñānamaīśhvaraṁ tatra yogastava |
Tatra nētā tadvidvān na prāpnoti lōkam ||

**Translation:**
"Astrology is divine knowledge, and yoga is its essence; the wise one attains the world through this knowledge."

---

## Shloka 2: Planetary Harmony Shloka
**Context:** The harmony of planets and their influences.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
ग्रहाणां सङ्घातो ज्ञानं तत्र योगः स्यात् |
संयोगात्सुखं दुःखं च तत्र विद्यते ||

**Transliteration:**
Grahāṇāṁ saṅghātō jñānaṁ tatra yōgaḥ syāt |
Sanyogātsukhaṁ duḥkhaṁ ca tatra vidyatē ||

**Translation:**
"The union of planets gives knowledge; yoga exists in that union; from connection comes happiness and sorrow."

---

## Shloka 3: House Effects Shloka
**Context:** The house-specific results.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
भवान्तराश्च तुल्येषु द्वादशेण प्रकाशितः |
प्रत्यन्तरं तत्र स्यात्सुखं दुःखं च प्रदाय ||

**Transliteration:**
Bhavaantarasyāc caturdō daśēna prakāshitāḥ |
Pratyantaraṁ tatra syāt sukhaṁ duḥkhaṁ ca pradāya ||

**Translation:**
"The houses arise by divisions; in each house, happiness and sorrow are given."

---

## Shloka 4: Marriage Predictions Shloka
**Context:** Predictions related to marriage.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
७मः भावः स्याद्धर्मं तत्र स्वामी स्याद् विवाह |
स्वामीं शुभांश्च मां बुध्यात्स्याद् विवाहस्य फलम् ||

**Transliteration:**
Saptamaḥ bhāvaḥ syāddharmaṁ tatra svāmī syād vivāha |
Svāmīṁ śubhāṁśca māṁ budhyāt syād vivāhasya phalam ||

**Translation:**
"The 7th house is auspicious for marriage; the lord of the 7th indicates marriage; when one knows the 7th, the marriage results."

---

## Shloka 5: Career Predictions Shloka
**Context:** Career and professional predictions.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
१०मः भावः स्याद्धर्मं तत्र प्रजापतिः स्यात् |
कर्मरतो भवेत्स्याद्धर्माद्धनमाहृते ||

**Transliteration:**
Daśamaḥ bhāvaḥ syāddharmaṁ tatra prapatthi syāt |
Karmarato bhavētysyāddharmāddhanamāhr̥tē ||

**Translation:**
"The 10th house brings dharma; there arises the lord of profession; one becomes devoted to work, and earnings follow."

---

## Shloka 6: Wealth Predictions Shloka
**Context:** Wealth and prosperity predictions.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
२-११ भावाः स्याद्धनं तत्र दानं च सुखदम् |
शुभं ग्रहांश्च मां बुध्यात्स्याद्धनस्य फलम् ||

**Transliteration:**
Dvitīyā-ekādaśa bhāvāḥ syāddhanaṁ tatra dānaṁ ca sukham |
Śubhaṁ grahāṁśca māṁ budhyāt syāddhanasya phalam ||

**Translation:**
"The 2nd and 11th houses give wealth, charity, and happiness; when one knows these, the results of wealth."

---

## Shloka 7: Health Predictions Shloka
**Context:** Health predictions.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
६मः भवान्तिर्हर्षाय स्वस्थो निवासते।
दुःखांश्च मां बुध्यात्स्याद्धर्माद्यात्र शान्तये ||

**Transliteration:**
Shadamaḥ bhāvāntir hariṣāyā svastho nivāsatē |
Duḥkhāṁśca māṁ budhyāt syāddharmādyātir śāntaye ||

**Translation:**
"The 6th house gives health and happiness; when one knows these, one becomes healthy by dharma."

---

## Shloka 8: Dasha System Shloka
**Context:** Planetary periods system.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
महादशा तु द्वादशवर्षाणां तु चतुर्थं येन |
तत्रैव दशां शासनम् स्यात्प्राप्नोति सुखमाहर्ति ||

**Transliteration:**
Mahādaśā tu dvādaśa varṣāṇāṁ tu caturthaṁ yena |
Tatraigra daśāṁ śāsanam syātprapnōti sukhaṁ āhrti ||

**Translation:**
"Mahadasha lasts twelve years; by the fourth part of the year, dasha is governed, and happiness is achieved."

## Note on Hindu Predictive Astrology by B.V. Raman
B.V. Raman (1900-1968) was a renowned Indian astrologer who wrote extensively on predictive astrology. His works include:

- Hindu Predictive Astrology
- Complete Book on Vedic Astrology
- Various articles on predictive techniques

His approach focused on:
- Combining classical texts with practical predictions
- Dasha and transit analysis
- House-by-house predictions
- Marriage, career, wealth, and health predictions

These shlokas represent the principles he emphasized in his writings.
