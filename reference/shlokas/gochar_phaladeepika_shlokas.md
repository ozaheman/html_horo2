# Reference: Shlokas for Gochar Phaladeepika (Transit Predictions)

## Overview
This document contains Sanskrit shlokas from the Gochar Phaladeepika text, focusing on planetary transits and their effects.

---

## Shloka 1: Lagna (Ascendant) Transit Shloka
**Context:** Effects of the ascendant lord's transit through different signs.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
लग्नस्वामिनो गोचरे भावान्यनुरुपनीतः |
स यः प्रदश्यते रामाय सोऽस्य स्वमाहरेत् ||

**Transliteration:**
Lagñasvamino gocare bhāvānyanurupanītaḥ |
Sa yaḥ pradakṣyate rāmāya so 'sya svamāharet ||

**Translation:**
"To the lord of the ascendant in transit, houses are established; whoever sees this, that is his eternal fruit."

---

## Shloka 2: Moon Transit Shloka
**Context:** Moon-based transit predictions and emotional effects.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
चंद्रमा तुल्यं हर्षाय सुखं विनाशयेत् |
त्रिकोणं योगं च यो द्रष्टुं प्रवृत्तिमनुयात् ||

**Transliteration:**
Candramā tulyaṁ harṣāya sukhaṁ vināśayet |
Trikoṇaṁ yogaṁ ca yo daṣṭuṁ pravṛttim anuayāt ||

**Translation:**
"The Moon gives happiness and destroys sorrow; whoever sees the trine yoga, he follows the fulfillment."

---

## Shloka 3: Jupiter Transit Shloka
**Context:** Effects of Jupiter transit (beneficial).

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
गुरुः शुभं भवेद्राज्यं दानं ददाति गुरवः |
तत्पुरुषं सुखं याति गुरोः प्रथमोऽथ यः ||

**Transliteration:**
Guruh śubhaṁ bhavērdrājyaṁ dānaṁ dadāti guravaḥ |
Tatpuruṣaṁ sukhaṁ yāti gurōḥ prathamō 'tha yaḥ ||

**Translation:**
"Jupiter causes royal fortune, gives charity; the first and the one who follows enjoy happiness in the service of Jupiter."

---

## Shloka 4: Saturn Transit Shloka
**Context:** Effects of Saturn transit (malefic).

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
शनிஃ कुर्यात् क्रियाद्यं दारुं दारं सुवर्णम् |
मत्त्वेव च दारं दुष्टं दुःखं याति शनैश्च ||

**Transliteration:**
Śanaiḥ kuryāt kriyādyam dāruṁ dāraṁ suvarṇam |
Matteva ca dāraṁ duṣṭaṁ duḥkhaṁ yāti śanaiśca ||

**Translation:**
"Saturn causes action, wealth, and sorrow; whatever is done, wealth, and evil results in pain."

---

## Shloka 5: Venus Transit Shloka
**Context:** Venus transit and love relationships.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
शुक्रः स्वप्नवादितिषु रक्तं रतिं च याति |
स्त्रीजन्मं ददाति संगं रतिं च सुखमाहृते ||

**Transliteration:**
Sukraḥ svapnavāditishu raktaṁ ratiṁ ca yāti |
Strījanaṁ dadāti saṅgaṁ ratiṁ ca sukhamāhr̥tē ||

**Translation:**
"Venus gives dreams, blood, and sex; it gives birth to women, and joins with them, and enjoyment is experienced."

---

## Shloka 6: Mercury Transit Shloka
**Context:** Mercury transit and communication/business.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
बुधः शरीरं चान्तर्घातम् मानसं भवेत् |
बुद्धिः कामादिलक्षणं वाणी चान्तर्घातम् ||

**Transliteration:**
Budhaḥ śarīraṁ cāntargrahāt mānasam bhavēt |
Buddhir kāmādikalpaṇaṁ vāṇī cāntargrahāt ||

**Translation:**
"Mercury affects the body, speech, and mind; intelligence and desires, and speech have these effects."

---

## Shloka 7: Mars Transit Shloka
**Context:** Mars transit and courage/conflict.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
मंगलो संप्राप्तेर्नित्यं दुःखात्मा युद्धशीलः |
शरीरे वातादिभिः संपदा च कुर्यादिम् ||

**Transliteration:**
Maṅgalō saṁprāpternityaṁ duḥkhātmā yuddaśīlaḥ |
Śarīre vātādbhiḥ saṃpadaṁ cu kurayād im ||

**Translation:**
"Mars is always present with pain, aggressive in war; in the body with wind and other ailments, and prosperity is achieved."

---

## Shloka 8: Rahu/Ketu Transit Shloka
**Context:** Rahu and Ketu transit effects (shadow planets).

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
राहुर्नक्षत्रं तुल्यं चेत् केतुः शरीरं स्थितम् |
राह्विमिस्तेषां तृतीयमास्रयं सर्वतः ||

**Transliteration:**
Rāhurnakṣatraṁ tulyaṁ cēt kētuḥ śarīraṁ sthitam |
Rāhvimisteṣāṁ tṛtīyam āsrayaṁ sarvataḥ ||

**Translation:**
"Rahu and Ketu are in the sign with the Moon; wherever they reside, they affect the third house and all planets."

## Note on Gochar Phaladeepika
The Gochar Phaladeepika text contains verses about:
- Transit effects of each planet in different houses
- Timing of events through transits
- Combination of transits with dashas
- Special significators for transits

These shlokas form the foundation of modern transit astrology.
