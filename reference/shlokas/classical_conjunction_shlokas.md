# Classical Conjunction Prediction References

The Predictions panel now matches the exact classical planets sharing a house against Saravali's conjunction chapters. A single occupant continues to use the existing planet-in-house result. Multi-planet results and their references are shown separately; unsupported sets are not assigned a generic count-based outcome.

## Source-text qualifications

- Chapter 17 has two entries (slokas 8 and 17) whose listed planet names contain only three planets despite the four-planet chapter heading. They are shown as source variants, not treated as four-planet formulas.
- Chapter 17 repeats the Moon-Mars-Jupiter-Saturn group in slokas 24-25 with differing descriptions. Both references are displayed.
- In Chapter 18, the Sun-Moon-Mercury-Jupiter-Saturn group appears in slokas 7-8 with differing descriptions, and the Moon-Mars-Mercury-Venus-Saturn group appears in slokas 13 and 20 with differing descriptions. Both references are displayed.
- The supplied extract does not provide exact matches for the four-planet sets Sun-Moon-Jupiter-Venus, Sun-Mercury-Jupiter-Venus, and Moon-Mars-Jupiter-Venus, or the five-planet set Sun-Moon-Mercury-Jupiter-Venus. These are left unpredicted and visibly marked for verification.
- No general count-only prediction is inferred for seven or more occupants. Rahu and Ketu are not part of these seven-classical-planet combination tables; when present alongside a matched group, the UI notes that limitation.

These are traditional textual indications, not deterministic outcomes or medical, financial, or relationship advice. The source extracts are in [saravali-1.md](../saravali-1.md).
