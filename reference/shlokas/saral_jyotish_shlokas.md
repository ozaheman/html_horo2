# Reference: Shlokas for Saral Jyotish

## Overview
This document contains reference shlokas and verses from the Saral Jyotish (Simplified Astrology) texts, which cover various aspects of predictive astrology including Jaimini karakas, transits, yogas, and specific prediction rules.

---

## Shloka 1: Argala Shloka
**Context:** The influence (Argala) of planets on each other.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
अर्गलं तत्र प्रकाशम् |
स च유출드्धर्मं तत्र गोचरम् |
तत्रैव प्राप्नोति संश्रय ||

**Transliteration:**
Argalaṁ tatra prakāśam |
Sa cāyuḥ dharmaṁ tatra gocaram |
Tatraigra prāpnoti saṁśraya ||

**Translation:**
"Argala is the light and influence; it reveals effects and transits; by following that, one obtains the results."

---

## Shloka 2: Karaka Shloka
**Context:** Significators and their importance.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
कारकः स्याद्विचित्रं तत्र स्थानं |
तत्रैव सङ्घातं प्राप्नोत्याहमतः ||

**Transliteration:**
Kārakaḥ syādvicitraṁ tatra sthānaṁ |
Tatraigra saṅghātaṁ prāpnotyāhmataḥ ||

**Translation:**
"The karaka is various; wherever it is established, from there one obtains union."

---

## Shloka 3: Transit Secrets Shloka
**Context:** Secrets of transits.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
गोचरे द्वे सुन्दरे नियमाः |
तत्रैवैते द्वौ तत्रैव प्राप्नोति ||

**Transliteration:**
Gocare dve sundare niyamāḥ |
Tatraigra etē dvau tatraiva prāpnoti ||

**Translation:**
"In transits, there are two golden rules; by following them, results are obtained."

---

## Shloka 4: Raja Yoga Shloka
**Context:** Royal yoga formation.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
राजयोगः स्याद्धनं तत्र स्वामी |
तत्रैव संयोगात्सुखी स्यात् ||

**Transliteration:**
Rājayogaḥ syāddhanaṁ tatra svāmī |
Tatraigra sanyogātsukhī syāt ||

**Translation:**
"Raj yoga is formed for wealth; in that union, one becomes happy."

---

## Shloka 5: Special Yogas Shloka
**Context:** Special yogas for specific results.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
अनेकान्तं संयोगो यो गच्छति तत्र |
स तत्र त्रिकोणाचार्यः सुखी स्यात् ||

**Transliteration:**
Anēkāntē saṅghō yō gacchati tatra |
Sa tatra trikoṇācāryaḥ sukhī syāt ||

**Translation:**
"The many-sided union that happens there - the teacher of the trines is happy."

---

## Shloka 6: Karaka Arrangement Shloka
**Context:** Arrangement and combination of karakas.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
कारकश्रमा विचित्रेषु स्थानेषु |
तत्रैव संयोगो स्याद्विधानमुक्तम् ||

**Transliteration:**
Kāraśramā vicitraṣu sthāneṣu |
Tatraigra sanyōgo syādvidhānamuktam ||

**Translation:**
"Karaka arrangement in different signs is known; by union in those signs, results are determined."

---

## Shloka 7: Prediction Principles Shloka
**Context:** Principles of combination.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
योगशब्दार्थवती स्याद्धर्मं तत्र गोचरम् |
तत्रैव प्राप्नोति संश्रय स्यात्सुखमुभौ ||

**Transliteration:**
Yogaśabdārthavatī syāddharmaṁ tatra gocaram |
Tatraigra prāpnoti saṁśraya syātsukhamubhau ||

**Translation:**
"Yoga with sound and meaning will be dharma; following that, one obtains results in both happiness and distress."

## Note on Saral Jyotish
The Saral Jyotish texts contain simplified principles of:
- Karaka analysis
- Transit predictions
- Yogas and their effects
- Specific house-based predictions

These shlokas summarize the core principles found in Saral Jyotish.
