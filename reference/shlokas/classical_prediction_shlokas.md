## Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra: Fifth lord in the tenth

**Source:** `reference/bookbph.md`, Chapter 24, verse 58, “Effects Of The Bhava Lords.” The extracted Markdown identifies the chapter and verse; it does not identify the exact printed page.

**Sanskrit (as transcribed in the source):**

> सुतेशे राज्यमावस्थे राजयोगो हि जायते।  
> अनेकसुखभोगी च स्यातकीतिर्नरो भवेत् ॥५८॥

**Meaning:** The text associates the fifth lord placed in the tenth with status and recognition. Its accompanying commentary says strength and affliction can change the result.

**Used for:** The conditional “Fifth lord in the tenth house” indication in the BPHS prediction report. Do not treat the verse as a guaranteed outcome.
