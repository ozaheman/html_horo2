# Classical Yoga Shloka References

This note records the source citations used by the chart-detectable classical yoga entries in `src/data/yogas_data.js`. The application keeps each citation in `referenceShloka`, separate from its prediction text.

## Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS), Chapter 35: Nabhasa Yogas

The supplied BPHS extract numbers the formation rules in shlokas 7-17 and the individual effects in shlokas 18-49. The seven Sankhya forms are suppressed when an earlier Nabhasa yoga is present, following shlokas 16-17.

| Yoga | Formation | Effects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rajju, Musala, Nala | 7 | 18, 19, 20 |
| Maala, Sarpa | 8 | 21, 22 |
| Gada, Sakata, Vihaga, Sringataka, Hala, Vajra, Yava | 9-11 | 23-29 |
| Kamala, Vapi | 12 | 30-31 |
| Yupa, Sara, Sakthi, Danda | 13 | 32-35 |
| Nauka, Koota, Chatra, Chapa | 14 | 36-39 |
| Chakra, Samudra | 15 | 41-42 |
| Vallaki/Veena, Daama, Paasa, Kedara, Soola, Yuga, Gola | 16-17 | 43-49 |

Ardhachandra is handled from Saravali for its formation rule; BPHS Chapter 35, shloka 40 supplies an effects reference.

## BPHS, Chapter 38: Solar Yogas

Vesi, Vosi/Vasi, and Ubhayachari use Chapter 38, shlokas 1-4 for their formation and effects.

## Saravali, Chapter 21: Nabhasa Yogas

Ardhachandra formation is cited to shloka 13: the seven classical planets occupy seven consecutive houses, with the first house of the span not angular from the Ascendant. Its effects are cited to shloka 25.
