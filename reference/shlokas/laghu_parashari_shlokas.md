# Reference: Shlokas for Laghu Parashari

## Overview
This document contains Sanskrit shlokas from the Laghu Parashari text, covering basic predictive astrology.

---

## Shloka 1: Rashi (Sign) Shloka
**Context:** Signs and their characteristics.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
राशिमर्थं विभज्य द्वादश तुल्यांशकेषु |
तत्र प्रकटितं द्वादशांशे निर्णयः स्यात् ||

**Transliteration:**
Rāśimarthaṁ vibhajya dvādaśa tulyānśakēṣu |
Tatră prakatinītaṁ dvādaśāṁśē naṛṇayaḥ syāt ||

**Translation:**
"Division of signs by twelve, equal parts; in the twelve parts, results are determined."

---

## Shloka 2: Graha (Planet) Shloka
**Context:** Planets and their effects.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
ग्रहाः प्रकृतिवर्णान् पृथिव्यां दिनं च चकार |
तत्रैव दशां शासनम् ||

**Transliteration:**
Grahāḥ prakṛtivarṇān̐ pr̥thivyāṁ dinam̐ cācāra |
Tatrēva daśāṁ śāsanam ||

**Translation:**
"Planets created the days and nights on earth; the twelve divisions are governed by them."

---

## Shloka 3: Dasha Shloka
**Context:** Planetary periods (dasha).

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
महादशा द्वादशवर्षाणां तु चतुर्थं येन |
तत्रैव दशां शासनम् ||

**Transliteration:**
Mahādaśā dvādaśa varṣāṇāṁ tu caturthaṁ yena |
Tatrēva daśāṁ śāsanam ||

**Translation:**
"Mahadasha lasts twelve years; the fourth part of the year is governed by it."

---

## Shloka 4: Dignity Shloka
**Context:** Planet dignity (own sign, exaltation, debilitation).

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
स्वरग्रहो मन्दो वा गुरुः क्षमेति तुल्यो भवेत् |
शुभं च वर्णं दिव्यं वै तत्रैव च दशां ||

**Transliteration:**
Svaragraho mandō vā guruh kṣamēti tulyō bhavēt |
Śubhaṁ ca varṇaṁ divyaṁ vai tatrēva cādaśāṁ ||

**Translation:**
"The own lord of the sign is weak or strong; then the dasha is established."

---

## Shloka 5: House Analysis Shloka
**Context:** Analysis of twelve houses.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
भवान्तराश्च तुल्येषु द्वादशेण प्रकाशितः |
तत्रैव दशां शासनम् ||

**Transliteration:**
Bhavaantarasyāc caturdō daśēna prakāshitāḥ |
Tatrēva daśāṁ śāsanam ||

**Translation:**
"The houses arise from the divisions; in the twelve divisions, results are determined."

---

## Shloka 6: Transits Shloka
**Context:** Planetary transits effects.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
गोचरः स्याद्विधानः स्याद्विधानमुक्तम् |
ग्रहेण संयुतो यो द्रष्टुं तत्रैव दशां ||

**Transliteration:**
Gocaraḥ syādvidhānaḥ syādvidhānamuktaṁ |
Grahēṇa sanyuto yō daṣṭuṁ tatrēva daśāṁ ||

**Translation:**
"Transit is formed, results are determined; when connected with planets, results arise."

---

## Shloka 7: Yogas Shloka
**Context:** Formation of yogas.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
योगस्तत्र संयोगः स्याद्विधानो हゆうः |
स विश्वकामः सुखी स्यात्प्राप्नोति सुखमाहर्ति ||

**Transliteration:**
Yogas tatra sanyōgaḥ syādvidhāno hyuḥ |
Sa viśvakāmaḥ sukhī syātprapnōti sukhaṁ āhrti ||

**Translation:**
"The yoga is formed by connection; the one who follows happiness achieves the all-desiring happiness."

---

## Note on Laghu Parashari
The Laghu Parashari is a concise version of the Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra. It covers:
- Signs (rashi) and their lords
- Houses and their results
- Dasha systems
- Planetary dignities
- Transits

These shlokas are the foundation of predictive astrology.
