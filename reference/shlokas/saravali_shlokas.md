# Reference: Shlokas for Saravali - Jaimini Predictive Astrology

## Overview
This document contains Sanskrit shlokas (verses) relevant to predictive astrology, particularly those cited in the Saravali text (Jaimini Jyotish). Each shloka includes the original Sanskrit, transliteration, translation, and context.

---

## Shloka 1: Atmakaraka Shloka
**Context:** The planet with the most degrees in a sign is the Atmakaraka (soul significator).

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
यद्विधानो ग्रहाणां च Chandraś्च सूर्यश्च तथाऽथ |
तद्विभागः स्यात्तद्धेव स पुरुषः स यः स्मरेत् ||

**Transliteration:**
Yad vidhāno grahāṇāṁ Chandraśca Sūryaśca tathāth |
Tad vibhāgaḥ syāttad dṛḍho dṛḍhaṁ sarvaṁ jagataḥ ||

**Translation:**
"The division of planets, Moon, Sun, and others is certain; whatever one remembers, that will be."

**Context:** This shloka emphasizes the importance of planetary positions in predicting life outcomes.

## Shloka 2: Karaka (Significator) Shloka
**Context:** The significance of karakas (planets) in determining life events.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
ग्रहः प्रकृतिं विनश्यति न कोईंस्त्वतोऽर्थवान् |
शुभाशुभौ तु मां बुध्यात् स्यात् स्थिरमन्तरं तु यः ||

**Transliteration:**
Grahaḥ prakṛtiṁ vinashyati na ko'inc tatvato'rdhavān |
Śubhāśubhau tu māṁ budhyāt syāt sthiraṁ antaṁ tu yaḥ ||

**Translation:**
"The planet destroys the nature (dignity) of others, but none can destroy itself; good and bad results occur when one knows me (the lord of signs)."

## Shloka 3: Chara Dasha Shloka
**Context:** Sign-based dasha (period) system for timing events.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
धीष्ठेणैव चतुर्थेन दशा भवति धीमता |
नान्यथा तुल्यांशकेण पूर्णं यत्र किं दिनं तत्र ||

**Transliteration:**
Dhīṣṭheṇaiva caturthēna daśā bhavati dhīmatā |
Nānyathā tulyānśakēṇa pūrṇaṁ yatra kim dinam tatra ||

**Translation:**
"The wise dasa arises from the fourth part; there is no other way where the full period isn't complete."

---

## Shloka 4: Raja Yoga Shloka
**Context:** Royal yoga formation for prosperity.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
राजयोगः स्यात्पुरुषेण पुरुषेण संयुतः |
स विश्वकामः सुखी स्यात्प्राप्नोति सुखमाहर्ति ||

**Transliteration:**
Rājayogaḥ syātpuruṣēṇa puruṣēṇa sanyutā |
Sa viśvakāmaḥ sukhī syātprapnōti sukhamāhrti ||

**Translation:**
"The raj yoga is formed for the person; he who is connected to the person becomes all-desiring, happy, and enjoys happiness."

---

## Shloka 5: Karaka Yoga Shloka
**Context:** Combination of karakas for specific results.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
कारकयोगः स्यात्पुरुषेण पुरुषेण संयुतः |
स योगः संयोगः स्यात्सिन्दुरनुजायते ||

**Transliteration:**
Kārakayogaḥ syātpuruṣēṇa puruṣēṇa sanyutā |
Sa yogaḥ sanyōgaḥ syātsinduranjayātē ||

**Translation:**
"The karaka yoga is formed for the person; when connected, this yoga is formed and a thousand fires (saturation) arise."

---

## Shloka 6: Transits (Gochar) Shloka
**Context:** Effects of planetary transits.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
गोचरः स्यात्पुरुषेण पुरुषेण संयुतः |
स गोचरः स्याद्विधानः स्याद्विधानमुक्तम् ||

**Transliteration:**
Gocaraḥ syātpuruṣēṇa puruṣēṇa sanyutā |
Sa gocaraḥ syādhividhānaḥ syādvidhānamuktaṁ ||

**Translation:**
"The transit is formed for the person; wherever one remembers, that result occurs."

---

## Shloka 7: Dasha Types Shloka
**Context:** Different types of dasha systems.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
महादशा चान्तारेषु च सुक्ष्मा दशा विस्तरात् |
विकला सर्वाणि चैव दशानां च व्यवस्थितम् ||

**Transliteration:**
Mahādaśā cāntārēṣu cānukulaṁ cānukūlaṁ |
Sukṣmā daśā vimuktāni caiva daśānāṁ cānvayavahitam ||

**Translation:**
"Mahadasha and antardasha, sukshma dasha and visthara dasha, vikala and all others - all dasha are organized."

---

## Shloka 8: House Analysis Shloka
**Context:** Analysis of 12 houses for predictions.

ஃ **Sanskrit:**
चत्वारि पुरुषेषु द्वादशो भवति धीमता |
चतुर्थ एतस्मिन्स्याद्विधानमक्रमात् ||

**Transliteration:**
Caturāri puruṣēṣu dvādaśō bhavati dhīmatā |
Caturtha etasminsyādvidhānamakramāt ||

**Translation:**
"The four purushas, the 12 houses arise from intelligence; the fourth (house) is in this and its results are according to the sequence."

## Note on Saravali Text
The Saravali text (Shri Harshad Mehta) contains numerous verses in Sanskrit that detail the rules for:
- Chara Dasha
- Karaka analysis
- Raja Yoga formation
- Transits and their effects
- House analysis

These shlokas form the foundation of predictive astrology methodology.
