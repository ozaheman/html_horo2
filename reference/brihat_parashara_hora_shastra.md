# Reference: Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra - Vol 1

## Overview
**Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra** is one of the most authoritative texts on Vedic predictive astrology, attributed to the sage Parashara. It covers deep predictive techniques including yogas, dasha, transits, divisional charts, and special timing methods.

## Key Prediction Rules from Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra

### 1. Fundamentals
- **Rashi**: 12 signs from Aries to Pisces
- **House**: 12 houses from the ascendant
- **Planet**: 9 planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu)
- **Dasha**: Planetary periods (17-year main period, sub-periods)

### 2. Dignity of Planets (Graha Charitra)
- **Swarastha (Own Sign)**:
  - Sun: Aries, Libra
  - Moon: Cancer
  - Mars: Aries, Scorpio
  - Mercury: Virgo, Pisces
  - Jupiter: Cancer, Sagittarius, Pisces
  - Venus: Taurus, Pisces
  - Saturn: Capricorn, Aquarius
- **Exaltation**:
  - Sun: Aries
  - Moon: Taurus
  - Mars: Scorpio
  - Mercury: Virgo
  - Jupiter: Cancer, Capricorn
  - Venus: Pisces
  - Saturn: Libra
- **Debilitation**: Opposite of exaltation
- **Neutral**: 5th house (Isanyan)

### 3. Dasha System Overview
- **Mahadasha**: 7-year periods divided among 7 planets
- **Antardasha**: Sub-periods (2, 3, 5, 8 years)
- **Pratyantardasha**: Sub-sub-periods
- **Sukshma Dasha**: Short-term (weeks)
- **Vikala Dasha**: Longer periods or gaps

### 4. Krishna (Malefic) and Shukla (Benefic) Planets
- **Krishna**: Saturn, Mars, Sun (when in 6th/8th/12th)
- **Shukla**: Jupiter, Venus, Mercury (when in 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th, 10th, 11th)
- A planet can be both Krishna and Shukla depending on placement

### 5. Transits (Gochar)
- Each planet transits all 12 signs during its period
- Transit effects are influenced by:
  - Planet's dignity
  - Its dasha lord position
  - Its aspect to other planets
  - Its house placement

### 6. Special Yogas
- **Jaimini Yoga**: Based on house placement and aspects
- **Chara Yoga**: Based on degree positions
- **Drekkana Yoga**: Based on 3rd division of signs
- **Navamsa Yoga**: Based on 9th division
- **Lagna Yoga**: Combination of strong planets at 1st house

### 7. Moon-based Prediction (Chandra)
- **Moon in 1st house**: Good health, prosperity
- **Moon in 2nd house**: Wealth, family happiness
- **Moon in 3rd house**: Courage, intelligence
- **Moon in 4th house**: Peace, comfort, mother
- **Moon in 5th house**: Wisdom, creativity, children
- **Moon in 6th house**: Challenges but wins through effort
- **Moon in 7th house**: Love, marriage
- **Moon in 8th house**: Transformation, longevity
- **Moon in 9th house**: Fortune, guidance, dharma
- **Moon in 10th house**: Career, authority
- **Moon in 11th house**: Wealth, gains, success
- **Moon in 12th house**: Spirituality, but may cause expenses

### 8. Graha Phala (Planet's Effects)
- **Sun**: Father, vitality, leadership, health
- **Moon**: Mind, emotions, mother, contentment
- **Mars**: Courage, energy, anger, rivalries
- **Mercury**: Intelligence, speech, communication, business
- **Jupiter**: Wisdom, fortune, father, auspiciousness
- **Venus**: Love, beauty, wealth, luxury, affection
- **Saturn**: Patience, discipline, obstacles, longevity

### 9. House by House Predictive Analysis
- **1st House (Lagna)**: Life, health, family, personality, fortune
- **2nd House**: Wealth, family, communication, food
- **3rd House**: Courage, siblings, short journeys, expression
- **4th House**: Home, mother, mind, property
- **5th House**: Children, education, intelligence, fortune
- **6th House**: Health, enemies, debts, litigation
- **7th House**: Marriage, partnerships, spouse
- **8th House**: Death, occult, sudden changes, inheritance
- **9th House**: Father, fortune, dharma, guidance
- **10th House**: Career, profession, father, reputation
- **11th House**: Gains, income, hopes, fulfillment
- **12th House**: Expenses, foreign travel, loss, spirituality

### 10. Prediction of Events
- Events are timed by dasha and transit interactions
- Strong planets give positive results
- Weak planets give negative results
- Benefic in kendras (1,4,7,10) = good; in maras (6,8,12) = bad
- Malefic in kendras = hard; in weak houses = very bad

### 11. Detailed House Analysis (Bhava Phala)
Each house has specific results that can be predicted through its lords:
- **Labha (Wealth)**: 2nd, 11th houses and their lords
- **Lakshana (Characteristics)**: 1st, 2nd, 5th, 9th houses and their lords
- **Upayoga (Special results)**: 5th house (children), 10th (career), 7th (marriage), 2nd (wealth), 6th (health)
- **Vedha (Curses/Woes)**: Malefic planets in 6th/8th/12th houses combined with malefic lords

### 12. Special Timing (Muhurta)
- **Gana**: Deity (Deva, Manushya, Rakshasa)
- **Tithi**: Lunar day (Krishna/Pratipada to Krishna/Purnima)
- **Vara**: Day of the week
- **Nakshatra**: Lunar mansion
