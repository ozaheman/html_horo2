# Reference: Saral Jyotish - Simplified Astrology Texts

## Overview
**Saral Jyotish** (Simplified Astrology) contains a collection of texts on various aspects of predictive astrology including Jaimini karakas, transits, yogas, and specific prediction rules.

## Key Concepts from Saral Jyotish Texts

### 1. Argala (Glow/Influence)
**Argala** refers to the light or influence emanating from a planet
- **Argala**: The aspect or influence of one planet on another
- **Vipreet Argala**: Reverse influence
- Argala can be auspicious or inauspicious depending on planet nature

### 2. Raja Yogas (Royal Yogas)
- **Rabhu Yoga**: 9th house from Lagna or Jupiter in 5th/9th
- **Dhana Yoga**: Wealth yoga
- **Lakshmi Yoga**: Prosperity
- **Uchchaishwyapa Yoga**: Moon, Jupiter, Saturn in 7th
- **Vashya Yoga**: Controlling yoga through Moon/Venus

### 3. Karakas (Significators)
- **Atmakaraka**: Soul purpose
- **Amatyakaraka**: Career
- **Darakaraka**: Spouse
- **Putrakaraka**: Children
- **Matrikaraka**: Mother
- **Bhratrikaraka**: Siblings
- **Gnatikaraka**: Enemies, disease
- **Pitrikaraka**: Father

### 4. Transit (Gochar) Secrets
- **2 Golden Rules** for transits:
  1. Check the house activated by the transiting planet
  2. Check the lords of those houses for yoga/afliction
- **Transit in D12**: House-wise transit effects
- Each planet activates houses based on its sign placement

### 5. Jaimini Principles
- **Jaimini Karka**: Births in a sign, not a house
- **Chara Dasha**: Sign-based periods
- **Karaka Yoga**: Based on karakas
- **Rashi Drishti**: Sign-based aspects

### 6. Special Yogas
- **Kangal Yoga (Kangaal Yog)**: Starving yoga - bad results
- **Tharai Yog**: Twelve-part yoga system
- **Maraka Yoga**: Death yoga
- **Asha Yog**: Hope yoga

### 7. Prediction Principles
- Combine **Dasha** (timing) + **Transit** (now) + **Yoga** (strength)
- Check planets in each house and their lords
- Karakas indicate what each house is about
- Strong planets give positive results, weak give negative

### 8. House Analysis
- **1st House**: Self, body, life
- **2nd House**: Wealth, family, speech
- **3rd House**: Courage, siblings, work
- **4th House**: Home, mother, peace
- **5th House**: Children, education, fortune
- **6th House**: Health, enemies, disease
- **7th House**: Marriage, partner, business
- **8th House**: Death, occult, sudden
- **9th House**: Father, fortune, dharma
- **10th House**: Career, profession, reputation
- **11th House**: Gains, income, hopes
- **12th House**: Expenses, loss, spirituality

### 9. Assessment of Results
- **Weak planets** in houses = problems
- **Strong planets** in houses = positive results
- **Benefics** in kendras/trikonas = excellent
- **Malefics** in maras = severe

### 10. Prediction of Marriage
- **7th House**: Marriage house
- **7th Lord**: Nature of partner
- **Venus**: Love, beauty
- **Moon**: Emotions
- **Jupiter**: Positive marriage
- **Mars**: Conflict
- **Saturn**: Late marriage
- **Rahu**: Multiple marriages

### 11. Prediction of Career
- **10th House**: Career, profession
- **10th Lord**: Professional fate
- **5th House**: Education
- **6th House**: Service
- **Planetary combinations**: Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, Venus, Sun
