So I am going to tell you my process that I follow for analyzing any Kundli. First of all, I need to draw a chart in which the degrees of all planets are also written
. Then along with that, I make a Navamsha list like this—not a chart, but a list
. After this, I write the strength (bal) of the planets in descending order
. Their values are available in the software, so thank God I don't have to calculate it every time, but everyone should know how to calculate it
.
Then I write down the Mahadasha, Antardasha, and Pratyantardasha
. And lastly, I write a few more things which are Atmakaraka, Karakamsa, Arudha Lagna, Amatyakaraka, and Upapada
. This is my playing field
. After setting this up, I will start making notes, not judgments
.
So, whichever house I am analyzing—for example, here is the 10th house—I write its Lord on the right, its Dispositor, and below that, its Navamsha Dispositor
. Now, I also write this information from the perspective of Sun, Moon, Karakamsa, and Arudha, and finally from the Dasha Lord as well
. This is my first line of information
.
Now I look at what the impacts of other planets are on them—meaning aspects (drishti) or conjunctions—and which houses these figured-out planets are in and what results they can give
. Also, I am a very visual thinker, so I make a lot of arrows and circles and sets
. This was my process; we will talk more about other processes in upcoming videos
