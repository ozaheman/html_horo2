# Reference: Laghu Parashari - Summary of Predictive Astrology

## Overview
**Laghu Parashari** (also known as Parashari Phala or Parashari Jyotish) is a concise version of the Brihat Parashari Hora Shastra. It covers the essential principles of Vedic predictive astrology including rashi (sign), house, dasha (planetary periods), and their combinations.

## Key Prediction Rules from Laghu Parashari

### 1. Fundamental Concepts
- **Rashi (Sign)**: 12 signs based on zodiac
- **House (Bhava)**: 12 houses from lagna
- **Planet (Graha)**: 9 planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu)
- **Dasha**: Planetary periods that time events

### 2. Rashi-based Predictions
- **1st House**: Self, personality, health, general fortune
- **2nd House**: Wealth, family, speech, food
- **3rd House**: Courage, siblings, short journeys
- **4th House**: Mother, home, mind, property
- **5th House**: Children, education, intelligence, fortune
- **6th House**: Disease, enemies, debts, litigation
- **7th House**: Spouse, business partners, marriage
- **8th House**: Death, longevity, occult, sudden gains
- **9th House**: Father, fortune, luck, dharma, guidance
- **10th House**: Career, profession, reputation, father
- **11th House**: Income, gains, hopes, fulfilment
- **12th House**: Foreign travel, expenses, loss, spiritual

### 3. House Lords
- Each house is ruled by a specific planet
- 1st: Sun (or Ascendant lord)
- 2nd: Venus
- 3rd: Mercury
- 4th: Moon
- 5th: Jupiter
- 6th: Saturn
- 7th: Venus
- 8th: Mercury
- 9th: Jupiter
- 10th: Saturn
- 11th: Venus
- 12th: Mercury

### 4. Dasha Systems (Periods)
- **Mahadasha**: 17-year major periods
- **Antardasha**: Sub-periods (5, 8, 10 years)
- **Pratyantardasha**: Sub-sub-periods
- **Sukshma Dasha**: Week-to-week period

### 5. Planet's Strength (Bal)
- **Sthan Bal (Positional Strength)**: 
  - Own sign = 50%
  - Exalted sign = 40%
  - Moderately strong = 30%
  - Afflicted = 20%
  - Debilitated = 10%
- **Drekkana (D3)**: 1/3 of sign
- **Dasha Strength**: Based on planet's position

### 6. Benefic and Malefic
- **Benefics**: Jupiter, Venus, Mercury (when well-placed), Moon
- **Malefics**: Saturn, Mars, Sun, Rahu, Ketu
- A planet can be both benefic and malefic depending on its placement
- Venus and Mercury are considered 2nd best in all

### 7. Aspects (Drishti)
- **Lagna**: 5th house aspect (but not 2nd/12th)
- **2nd House**: 9th house aspect (not 1st/3rd)
- **3rd House**: 11th house aspect (not 2nd/4th)
- **4th House**: 7th house aspect (not 3rd/5th)
- **5th House**: 9th house aspect (not 4th/6th)
- **6th House**: 12th house aspect (not 5th/7th)
- **7th House**: 4th house aspect (not 6th/8th)
- **8th House**: 2nd house aspect (not 7th/9th)
- **9th House**: 5th house aspect (not 8th/10th)
- **10th House**: 3rd house aspect (not 9th/11th)
- **11th House**: 1st house aspect (not 10th/12th)
- **12th House**: 6th house aspect (not 11th/1st)

### 8. Raja Yogas
- **Sanyoga Yoga**: Combination of two strengths
- **Shubha Yoga**: Combination of benefic and 3rd/5th/7th/9th from lagna
- **Dharma Yoga**: Jupiter in 5th or 9th
- **Dhan Yoga**: Venus in 7th from 5th or 5th in 7th
- **Uchchaishwyapa Yoga**: Moon, Jupiter, Saturn in 7th from lagna

### 9. Marriage Predictions (Specific to Laghu Parashari)
- **Moon in 7th**: Early marriage
- **Jupiter in 7th**: Happy marriage, 1-2 children
- **Mars in 7th**: Marriage problems, 3rd marriage
- **Venus in 7th**: Attractive spouse, happy married life
- **Saturn in 7th**: Late marriage, multiple marriages
- **Mercury in 7th**: Single spouse, multiple marriages
- **Sun in 7th**: Choleric spouse, married to someone superior
- **Rahu in 7th**: Multiple marriages, divorce
- **Ketu in 7th**: Multiple marriages, short-lived spouse

### 10. Career Predictions
- **10th house lord**: Career, profession
- **5th house**: Education, intellectual work
- **6th house**: Service, employment
- **2nd house**: Business, trade

### 11. Health Predictions
- **1st house**: General health
- **2nd house**: Throat, teeth, eyes
- **3rd house**: Hands, shoulders, nerve
- **4th house**: Stomach, mind
- **5th house**: Heart, brain
- **6th house**: Disease, injury
- **7th house**: Marriage health
- **8th house**: Death, transformation
- **9th house**: Father's health
- **10th house**: Career, father
- **11th house**: Income, health of relatives
- **12th house**: Expenses, foreign health

### 12. Prediction Principles
- Strong benefic planets in kendras/trikonas = good things
- Weak malefics in maras = suffering
- Dasha lord's placement determines the activities during that period
